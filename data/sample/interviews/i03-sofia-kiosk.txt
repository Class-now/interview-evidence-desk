# i03 - Sofia, coffee kiosk in a train station, 3 staff (SYNTHETIC)
Interviewer: How do you organise shifts?
Sofia: There are three of us. The rota is the same every week, I change it maybe once a month.
Sofia: It takes me ten minutes. It's not a problem at all.
Interviewer: What about last-minute changes?
Sofia: If someone is sick I just work the shift myself. That's the deal when you're this small.
Interviewer: Tell me about the last time that happened.
Sofia: Two weeks ago, I did a double shift. Tiring, but it's once in a while.
Interviewer: Have you ever looked for an app?
Sofia: No. I would not pay a monthly fee for three people, even five euros feels like too much.
Interviewer: Can your staff swap among themselves?
Sofia: They already do. They tell me after, and that's fine, I trust them completely.
Interviewer: Anything that does annoy you about staffing?
Sofia: Hiring. Finding someone reliable for weekends takes me weeks. That's my real headache.
