# i04 - Tomas, brunch restaurant, 18 staff incl. kitchen (SYNTHETIC)
Interviewer: How does scheduling work for you?
Tomas: I use a scheduling app, I pay 89 euros a month for it, and I've had it for two years.
Interviewer: How much time do you still spend on the rota?
Tomas: Building it, about an hour a week now, the app copies last week. Before the app it was a full evening.
Interviewer: What is still painful?
Tomas: Swaps. The app lets people offer shifts, but half my staff ignore the notifications.
Tomas: Last Saturday two people swapped in the app and nobody noticed one of them was a trainee. We were short at the grill.
Interviewer: Do you approve swaps?
Tomas: I switched approvals off last year because it was too much clicking. That Saturday made me regret it.
Interviewer: Would a cheaper tool that only handles swaps interest you?
Tomas: I already pay for the big one. I won't add another subscription just for swaps.
Interviewer: What would make you switch?
Tomas: If it understood skills, like who can work the grill alone, and nagged people until the shift is covered.
Tomas: I would switch tomorrow for that, even at the same price.
