# i05 - Amina, tea room and pastry shop, 8 staff (SYNTHETIC)
Interviewer: Walk me through your week of scheduling.
Amina: Monday morning I print a template, fill it in pencil, then photograph it for the staff group.
Amina: Then I redo it at least twice during the week. Altogether it's four or five hours, easily.
Interviewer: What triggers the redoing?
Amina: Students. Their exam timetables change, and they tell me on Wednesday for Thursday.
Interviewer: Tell me about the most recent one.
Amina: Last week three people asked for changes on the same day. I spent my whole break on the phone.
Interviewer: How do you feel about staff arranging swaps themselves?
Amina: I would love that. They're adults. I just need the final version somewhere I can see it.
Interviewer: Have you spent money on solving this?
Amina: I bought a big whiteboard and magnets, 60 euros. It helps in the shop but not when I'm at home.
Interviewer: What's the budget for something better?
Amina: I would pay around 15 euros a month. Twenty-five would be too much for us this year.
Amina: Margins on tea are thin, and the energy bill doubled.
