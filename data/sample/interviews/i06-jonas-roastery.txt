# i06 - Jonas, roastery cafe, 9 staff (SYNTHETIC)
Interviewer: How do you handle the rota?
Jonas: My shift lead, Ines, does it. I honestly don't know how long it takes her.
Interviewer: Have you ever asked?
Jonas: She once said Sunday nights are gone for it. So, three hours maybe.
Interviewer: What happens when someone can't make it?
Jonas: Ines handles it. Last month she worked eleven days in a row covering gaps, and then she quit.
Jonas: Replacing her took six weeks and a 400-euro job ad. That's when I realised it was a problem.
Interviewer: Are you looking for a tool now?
Jonas: Yes. I started a free trial of a scheduling app last week. We'll see.
Interviewer: How much would you expect to pay?
Jonas: I'd pay 30 a month without thinking if it keeps my next shift lead from burning out.
Interviewer: Should staff swap without approval?
Jonas: Yes, as long as the shift lead gets a summary. Approving each one is exactly what burned Ines out.
