# i07 - Claire, creperie, 5 staff, seasonal (SYNTHETIC)
Interviewer: How do you plan the staff?
Claire: In summer it's chaos, in winter it's me and one person. The summer rota takes me maybe two hours a week.
Interviewer: And changes?
Claire: Summer staff are tourists' kids, they cancel by text all the time. But I always have someone on call.
Interviewer: Tell me about the last cancellation.
Claire: August, a Saturday. I called my on-call guy, he came in twenty minutes. No drama.
Interviewer: Do you use any software?
Claire: A free calendar app. I wouldn't pay for scheduling, the season is only four months.
Interviewer: Would you let staff swap freely?
Claire: No. I've had a swap where the replacement was a friend who had never worked here. Never again.
Claire: Every swap goes through me, I have to know who is behind my counter.
