# i08 - Ravi, doughnut shop with delivery, 11 staff (SYNTHETIC)
Interviewer: How do you build the rota?
Ravi: I export last week from the till, adjust it in a spreadsheet, about two hours on Sunday.
Interviewer: What breaks during the week?
Ravi: Delivery riders. They swap between themselves on a group chat and I find out when an order is late.
Ravi: Last Friday we refunded 14 orders, around 230 euros, because two riders both thought the other was coming.
Interviewer: So do you want to approve swaps?
Ravi: I don't want to approve them, I want to know they're real. A swap should only count when both people confirm.
Interviewer: Have you paid for anything to fix it?
Ravi: I pay a student 50 euros a month to be the person who checks the chat every morning.
Ravi: If software did that job I'd pay the same 50 and keep the student for packing.
Interviewer: Anything you would not want?
Ravi: Another app for staff to install. They have WhatsApp, that's it. Anything else they ignore.
